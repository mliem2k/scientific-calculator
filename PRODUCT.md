# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students using a phone one-handed, in class or exams, entering expressions quickly and reading results at a glance. An Astro landing page (landing/) serves people who find the app.

## Product Purpose

A scientific calculator with a natural textbook display (fractions, roots, powers rendered as written), history, themes and settings. Success is fast, error-free entry and an unambiguous, readable result.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, precise. References: the ColorOS calculator for flat rounded keys and a single green accent, and Desmos for large type.

## Anti-references

1. Cluttered Casio-clone: tiny labels, every function visible at once.
2. Generic Material default: stock buttons, elevation, purple seed color (Material is used only for dialogs and switches, restyled).
3. SaaS-style landing: hero metrics, identical feature card grids, gradient text.
4. Neon or glossy gamer calculator: glow, gradients, saturated key colors.

## Design Principles

1. The display is the hero: the expression and result get the most space and the highest contrast.
2. Group keys by frequency and meaning; rare functions are one step away, not all visible.
3. One-handed reach: frequent keys sit in the thumb zone with generous targets.
4. Restrained color: neutrals plus one accent for the primary action (equals) and state.
5. Landing page tells the truth about the app, with no template scaffolding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 text, 3:1 large), 48dp minimum touch targets, layouts that survive 1.5x and larger system font scale, screen reader labels on every key, and reduced motion respected.
